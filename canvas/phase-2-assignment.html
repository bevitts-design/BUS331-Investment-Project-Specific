BUS331 · Phase 2 approval gate

BUS331 Investment Committee · Phase 2 Gate · Build and Challenge

Approve, revise, or reject each client portfolio after the locked baseline, instructor-issued Scenario Reveal, full IPS review, corrective trade, and re-test.

Submission process

Phase 2 has two checkpoints. At the instructor-designated baseline checkpoint, submit the Baseline Snapshot before the Scenario Reveal. After the reveal, complete the corrective trade and re-test, then submit the complete Gate 2 package. One designated team member submits for the group; all five members verify the Canvas receipt.

Canvas controls the due date, points, availability window, and attempt policy.

Required files

Completed capital-market-expectations and optimization workbook.

BUS331_Team##_Phase2_SecuritySelection.xlsx

Completed final-holding scorecards, selective decision notes, Holding & Exposure Reality Checks, conditional direct-security evidence, and FactSet Evidence Log.

BUS331_Team##_Phase2_StressTest.xlsx

BUS331_Team##_Phase2_DecisionRecord.xlsx

Current committee record with client-by-client votes, dissent, actions, owners, and deadlines.

BUS331_Team##_Phase2_LicensedEvidence.zip

Private course-use evidence files referenced by the FactSet Evidence Log; no credentials or unrelated exports.

Pre-submission check

  • Each client portfolio contains 8–10 holdings and never more than 10; funds and ETFs are primary, individual securities total no more than two or three, and no slot is filled without a purpose.
  • Every final holding has the concise scorecard, realistic alternatives are rejected briefly, and direct individual securities include rationale, idiosyncratic-risk, and position-size evidence.
  • The Portfolio Manager recorded overlap, concentration, final-selection, weight, and trade-off decisions; the Risk and Derivatives Analyst recorded residual risk and a supported hedge or no-hedge conclusion.
  • The baseline snapshot was submitted before the Scenario Reveal; every client allocation totals 100%, all IPS objectives and constraints were re-tested, and every revealed breach has a corrective trade and full re-test.
  • All five members recorded the Phase 2 vote and can identify their recommendation, alternative rejected, key trade-off, and evidence reviewed.
