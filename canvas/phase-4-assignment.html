BUS331 Investment Committee · Phase 4 · Integrated Presentation

Present the economic review, client mandates, security research and portfolio analysis as one evidence-linked recommendation for all three clients.

Submission process

Submit one team PowerPoint file through Canvas before the scheduled oral presentation. Deliver the presentation and Q&A with shared speaking responsibilities; every team member confirms the Canvas receipt. Use the completed Phase 3 workbook and prior research as evidence.

Canvas controls the due date, points, availability window, and attempt policy.

Required files

Use the team name entered on Team Information (or Summary in the Phase 3 workbook) in every filename. Replace [TeamName] with that name, omitting spaces and punctuation (for example, Harbor Analysts becomes HarborAnalysts); use the same spelling in every submission. Do not add individual student names to filenames.

Pre-submission check

  • Slides match the final Phase 3 workbook and identify sources and assumptions.
  • Each of the three client recommendations follows from the economic outlook, IPS, research and risk analysis.
  • Every member can explain their contribution and defend the integrated recommendation.
  • The team has checked the Canvas upload and is ready for the oral presentation.
