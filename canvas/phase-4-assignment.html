BUS331 Investment Committee · Phase 4 · Integrated Presentation

Present the economic review, client mandates, security research and portfolio analysis as one evidence-linked recommendation for all three clients.

Submit one team PowerPoint file through Canvas before the scheduled oral presentation. Deliver the presentation and Q&A with shared speaking responsibilities; every team member confirms the Canvas receipt. Use the completed Phase 3 workbook and prior research as evidence.

What your presentation must cover

  1. Lead with the recommendations: State the final recommendation for each of your three assigned clients and explain why it fits that client.
  2. Connect the economic review and client mandates: Explain the market outlook and key assumptions, then connect each IPS to return objectives, risk tolerance, liquidity, time horizon and other relevant constraints. Identify material information gaps and labeled assumptions.
  3. Show the final portfolios and supporting research: Present the 10 distinct securities per client and final weights totaling 100%. Explain each holding’s role and client fit, why funds and ETFs are the primary vehicles, and why any limited individual security earns its place. Use research to address costs, liquidity, overlap and concentration; cite sources and dates.
  4. Explain portfolio construction and judgment: Use clear exhibits from Phase 3 to compare initial, constrained minimum-variance Solver and final allocations. Explain the expected return and risk evidence, constraints, Solver outcome, alternatives rejected and key trade-offs behind the final weights.
  5. Defend the risk response: Show the downside or bear-case result for each client, the relevant IPS tripwire and client consequence, any corrective action and re-test, remaining risk, and a targeted hedge or supported no-hedge conclusion. Acknowledge the limits of the analysis.
  6. Close with implementation and monitoring: Identify next actions, monitoring triggers, unresolved reservations and the conditions that would cause the team to change the recommendation.

Speaking responsibilities and committee defense

Share speaking responsibilities. Include a compact defense covering all five committee roles. Each student answers at least one targeted question; on a four-person team, the dual-role member covers both owned roles. Each member must explain their evidence, recommendation, alternative rejected, key trade-off, Phase 3 workbook contribution, verification and limitations, and whether the challenge changes the decision.

Every member must be ready to defend the market view, client suitability, allocation, security choices, stress results and trade-offs. Questions may be directed to a member who did not lead that workstream. Use evidence and calculations, acknowledge uncertainty, and defend or revise your judgment under challenge.

  • Client and Macro Strategist: Defend client suitability, the macro assumptions, and the line from scenario-based need to recommendation.
  • Fixed-Income Analyst: Defend the portfolio's income, capital-preservation, interest-rate, and credit-risk trade-offs.
  • Equity Analyst: Defend equity exposures, vehicle choices, limited individual-security decisions, rejected alternatives, and their client fit.
  • Portfolio Manager: State and defend the integrated recommendation, portfolio construction decisions, final weights, rejected integrations, and implementation priorities.
  • Risk and Derivatives Analyst: Defend the stress evidence, residual risk, hedge or no-hedge decision, limits of the analysis, and conditions that would change the risk response.

Visual evidence and presentation schedule

Use legible, client-focused charts, tables and other exhibits that make the recommendation and supporting evidence easy to follow. All figures, holdings, weights and risk results must match the final Phase 3 workbook. Identify sources, dates and assumptions.

Follow the presentation schedule and any duration or slide-count directions announced in Canvas or class. The current project materials do not specify a fixed presentation length or required slide count.

How the presentation is assessed

BUS331 Investment Project Presentation and Defense (100-point rubric). Canvas controls the assignment points and course-grade weight.

  • Role-specific committee defense — 25%: Within the presentation's compact investment-committee defense, each member answers targeted questions for their role, uses relevant evidence and calculations, acknowledges uncertainty, and defends or revises the judgment under challenge.
  • Individual evidence & workbook contribution — 20%: Each member demonstrates command of owned evidence and can explain their recommendation, alternative rejected, key trade-off, workbook contribution, verification, and limitations.
  • Shared portfolio quality & integration — 25%: The committee presents one coherent chain from client scenario analysis through the 10-security-per-client selection, final weights, overlap/concentration decisions, stress results, residual-risk conclusion, correction, and final recommendation.
  • Client suitability & risk compliance — 20%: Members can show where client needs changed the portfolio, explain the strongest bear case and residual risk, and defend compliance, corrective action, and the residual-risk conclusion.
  • Clarity, professionalism & visual evidence — 10%: The team communicates concisely as client-focused professionals and uses legible exhibits that make the recommendation, trade-offs, and evidence visible.

Pre-submission check

  • Slides match the final Phase 3 workbook and identify sources and assumptions.
  • Each of the three client recommendations follows from the economic outlook, IPS, research and risk analysis.
  • Every member can explain their contribution and defend the integrated recommendation.
  • The team has checked the Canvas upload and is ready for the oral presentation.
