Step 1: Consider RRTTLLLU for each client

Read the assigned profile and matching client-data row. Think about risk, return, time horizon, taxes, liquidity, legal constraints, and unique circumstances. Consider risk capacity and willingness, including utility and risk aversion. Use supplied facts; mark missing information “Not provided.”

Step 2: Complete one abbreviated IPS for each client

Use the abbreviated IPS template for each of your three clients. Complete its short sections with concise sentences or bullets. The template guides your return and risk policies and provisional allocation totaling 100%. Record your RRTTLLLU reasoning in the IPS itself. No client interviews, signatures, contract, or approval gate are required.
