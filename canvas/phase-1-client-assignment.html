BUS331 Investment Committee · Phase 1 Part 2 · Client Analysis and Gate 1

Approve or revise the three client mandates using the submitted macro forecast before portfolio construction begins.

Submission process

Complete the IPS template from the assigned fictional scenario. Missing facts may remain Not provided; label essential modeling assumptions and justify committee policy choices. No client interviews are required. Use the macro forecast already submitted in Part 1; do not upload it again. After the Gate 1 vote, submit the three client-analysis files as the Part 2 group assignment. One designated team member submits for the group; every team member verifies the Canvas receipt.

Canvas controls the due date, points, availability window, and attempt policy.

Required files

Use the team name entered on Team Information in every filename. Replace [TeamName] with that name, omitting spaces and punctuation (for example, Harbor Analysts becomes HarborAnalysts); use the same spelling in every submission. Do not add individual student names to filenames.

BUS331_[TeamName]_Phase1_MandateMemo.pdf

One-page committee mandate memo covering the submitted market view and three client mandates.

BUS331_[TeamName]_Phase1_ClientIPS.pdf

One combined PDF containing the three completed client IPS documents in assigned-client order.

BUS331_[TeamName]_Phase1_DecisionRecord.xlsx

Current Analyst Decision Log with five-role-by-three-client coverage and one Gate 1 vote per team member.

Pre-submission check

  • The mandate memo identifies the submitted macro forecast and explains any revision since Part 1.
  • Each client has an objective, RRTTLLU constraints, return target, volatility limit, drawdown tripwire, and unresolved information gaps.
  • Each IPS distinguishes supplied scenario facts from labeled, reasoned assumptions; unsupported exact values are marked for confirmation.
  • Each IPS includes a provisional strategic allocation totaling 100% and policy-level implementation; specific securities, trades, and final optimized weights belong to Phase 2.
  • The Decision Record shows initial judgment, committee challenge, human verification, final reasoning, downstream guardrail, owner, and peer reviewer.
  • Every team member recorded one Phase 1 vote and any revision action; all five role responsibilities are covered.
