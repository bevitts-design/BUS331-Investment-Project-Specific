BUS331 Investment Committee · Phase 1 Part 2 · Client IPS Statements

Explain how each client’s criteria, risk, return, utility, and risk aversion shape a provisional asset allocation.

Submission process

Review the supplied client profiles and data, discuss risk, return, utility, and risk aversion, and complete one abbreviated IPS per client. Combine all three IPS statements into one PDF in assigned-client order. Submit only this PDF for Part 2; no separate mandate memo, Decision Log, or Gate 1 vote is required. Use the macro forecast already submitted in Part 1 as context. No client interviews are required. One designated team member submits for the group; every member verifies the Canvas receipt.

Canvas controls the due date, points, availability window, and attempt policy.

Pre-submission check

  • The PDF contains one completed abbreviated IPS for each assigned client, in assigned-client order.
  • Each IPS uses the supplied criteria and explains how risk, return, utility, and risk aversion influence asset allocation.
  • Each broad allocation totals 100% and respects the client’s goals, time horizon, liquidity needs, and restrictions.
  • Missing facts remain Not provided; proposed policies and any essential assumptions are clearly identified.
  • The three statements are legible, consistent with the submitted macro outlook, and ready to guide Phase 2.
