BUS331 Investment Committee · Phase 1 Part 1 · Macro Forecast

Submit the common 12-month macro forecast before applying it to assigned clients. The separate Part 2 assignment completes the three client IPS statements.

Submission process

Complete and submit the macro starter workbook as the Part 1 group assignment. One designated team member submits for the group; every team member verifies the Canvas receipt. Carry the submitted forecast into Part 2 and document any later revision in the decision record.

Canvas controls the due date, points, availability window, and attempt policy.

Required files

Use the team name entered on Team Information in every filename. Replace [TeamName] with that name, omitting spaces and punctuation (for example, Harbor Analysts becomes HarborAnalysts); use the same spelling in every submission. Do not add individual student names to filenames.

BUS331_[TeamName]_Phase1_Macro.xlsx

Completed macro starter workbook with historical evidence, human-first read, source-dated consensus comparison, inflation forecast, and probability-weighted bull/base/bear scenarios.

Pre-submission check

  • All five macro responsibilities have named owners; a four-person team may assign two responsibilities to one person.
  • Historical claims use the labeled series, frequency, units, and source dates; current observations are distinguished from 12-month forecasts.
  • GDP growth, CPI inflation, fed funds, and sentiment forecasts include a comparable external reference, source, date, horizon, and rationale when available; an unavailable comparison is explained.
  • Bull, base, and bear probabilities total 100%, and the weighted forecast calculates from the three scenarios.
