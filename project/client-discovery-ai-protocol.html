Open the four-step client guide

From client criteria to IPS

1

Review client criteria

Read the assigned goals, constraints, target return, standard deviation, and risk aversion.

2

Consider risk and return preferences

Discuss risk capacity and willingness. Utility reflects the client’s return–risk trade-off; greater risk aversion gives risk a larger penalty.

3

Explain asset allocation

Propose broad equity, fixed-income, and cash weights totaling 100%, supported by the client criteria and submitted macro outlook.

4

Complete the abbreviated IPS

Complete one concise IPS per client, review the three statements together, and submit one combined PDF.
