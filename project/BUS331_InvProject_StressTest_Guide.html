Portfolio Stress Testing and Residual Risk

Test the integrated portfolio, identify the client consequence, and record a supported risk response without assuming a derivative is required.

Carry forward the approved assumptions

Use the approved base and bear assumptions, current final weights, and every relevant IPS objective and constraint. Do not soften a scenario input, client tripwire, or portfolio exposure to manufacture a passing result.

Portfolio-level test

Measure the risk the client actually owns

  • Confirm weights total 100% and reconcile to the hypothetical AUM.
  • Measure the required return and course risk metrics consistently.
  • Identify concentration, overlap, liquidity, duration, credit, sector, style, and other material exposures.
  • Show both percentage and dollar consequences.
  • Name the client goal or constraint most exposed.
  • Classify every test as Pass, Warning, or Breach and state the limitation of the estimate.

Risk response

Correct a breach and re-test the full portfolio

For each Breach, record the holding or sleeve changed, old and revised weights, expected benefit, new trade-off, and revised return, risk, liquidity, and bear-case results. Re-run the complete IPS scorecard; narrative alone cannot cure a breach.

Residual-risk conclusion

Choose hedge or no hedge from evidence

No hedge

Identify the residual risk, explain why the portfolio should retain it, and show why cost, liquidity, complexity, basis risk, or another trade-off makes a hedge inferior.

One targeted hedge

Identify the residual risk, instrument, size, cost, liquidity, trade-offs, risk remaining, and the condition for adjustment, removal, or expiration.

Changing underlying holdings or weights may be the better risk response. A derivative is optional and must never be added for presentation theater.

Decision rights

Record the handoff

  • Portfolio Manager: owns integrated selection, final weights, overlap/concentration resolution, corrective trades, and explicit portfolio trade-offs.
  • Risk and Derivatives Analyst: owns portfolio stress evidence, residual-risk diagnosis, hedge or no-hedge conclusion, and the post-adjustment re-test.
  • Full committee: reviews the evidence and records five votes, dissent or reservations, actions, and monitoring triggers.
