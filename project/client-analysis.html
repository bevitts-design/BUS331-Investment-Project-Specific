Review the clients, then complete their IPS statements

  1. 1

    Review each client’s criteria

    Read your assigned team’s profile slides and the matching rows in the client-data workbook. Identify each client’s goals, time horizon, liquidity needs, restrictions, target return, standard deviation, and risk-aversion value. Use the supplied facts; write “Not provided” when information is missing.

  2. 2

    Consider risk, return, utility, and risk aversion

    Discuss how much risk the client can afford and how much risk the client is willing to accept. Is the target return reasonable given the client’s goals and risk? Utility describes the satisfaction a client receives from the return–risk trade-off. A higher risk-aversion value means a larger penalty for risk, so a higher-return portfolio is not automatically the preferred choice. Standard deviation describes volatility; it is not a maximum possible loss.

  3. 3

    Explain the asset-allocation implications

    Decide how those criteria should influence the mix of equity, fixed income, and cash. Consider how near-term cash needs, a longer investment horizon, and greater or lower risk aversion affect that mix. Use your submitted macro outlook as context. Propose broad weights totaling 100% and explain the main trade-off in a few sentences. Specific holdings and final portfolio optimization come in Phase 2.

  4. 4

    Complete one abbreviated IPS for each client

    Make one copy of the abbreviated IPS template for each of your three clients. Complete the six short sections using your discussion: client goals, return and risk policy, relevant constraints, provisional allocation, review rules, and open issues. Explain your proposed return objective, volatility limit, and drawdown review trigger briefly. Keep responses concise; bullets are welcome. Distinguish supplied facts from your proposed policies and any necessary assumptions. No client interview or signature is required.

Team review and submission

Compare your three IPS statements. Can you explain why the clients may need different allocations? Check that each allocation totals 100%, respects the stated constraints, and matches the client’s risk and return preferences.

The current submission requirements also include the one-page mandate memo and Decision Record. Summarize the three mandates and submitted macro view in the memo. Record each role’s client reasoning in the Analyst Decision Log, peer-review the entries, and record one Gate 1 vote per team member on PHASE 1. Resolve required revisions before beginning Phase 2.

Combine the three IPS statements in assigned-client order as BUS331_[TeamName]_Phase1_ClientIPS.pdf.
