Phase 1 · Mandate

Frame the Mandate

What must be true about markets and each client before we are willing to invest?

Approval gateApprove or revise the common market view and all three client mandates. Portfolio construction cannot begin until the vote and action items are recorded.

Market view

Read the historical data, compare the committee view with FactSet/FRED consensus, and build bull/base/bear scenarios.

Client discovery

Make a human-first judgment, complete the structured client role-play, and translate verified needs into RRTTLLU constraints.

Decision log and guardrails

Use a committee challenge to test the initial view, verify material claims, record final reasoning, and set criteria for later bonds, mutual funds/ETFs, allocation, and risk.

Phase 2 · Portfolio

Build & Challenge

Does each portfolio earn its place inside the client mandate after costs, constraints, and the instructor-issued Scenario Reveal?

Approval gateApprove, revise, or reject each client portfolio. The baseline snapshot must precede the Scenario Reveal, and a revealed breach cannot receive approval without a documented corrective trade and re-test.

A · Capital-market expectations

Convert the market view into expected returns, volatility, correlations, and Solver constraints for base and bear cases.

B · Securities & implementation

Compare and select bonds, mutual funds, and ETFs using instrument-specific evidence, client guardrails, costs, diversification, and a verified AI challenge.

C · Scenario Reveal & corrective action

Submit the baseline, apply the instructor-issued adverse-event packet, test the tripwire, quantify client impact, and document the trades required to cure each breach.

Phase 3 · Decision

Defend the Recommendation

Can every committee member defend the same evidence-linked recommendation to the panel?

Approval gateIssue the final recommendation, record dissent or reservations, and defend the evidence. Every member must answer questions outside the workstream they led.

Decision memo

State the recommendation first, then connect the market view, client mandate, portfolio, and risk decision.

Technical exhibit book

Present the auditable evidence: scenarios, IPS constraints, allocations, securities, stress results, and sources.

Committee defense

Deliver a visual pitch, answer adversarial questions, and show that all four members understand the integrated decision.

Committee operating protocol

Every phase ends with a decision

Analysis becomes committee work only when the team challenges it, votes, and records what happens next.

Pre-read

Circulate the required evidence before the meeting. Every member reviews the full package.

Recommendation

The workstream lead states a clear recommendation and cites the evidence that supports it.

Independent challenge

The Portfolio Manager and Risk Analyst coordinates the strongest counterargument; the analyst whose judgment is challenged must respond with evidence.

Motion and vote

The phase lead states the motion. The Portfolio Manager and Risk Analyst records approve, revise, or reject; all four members vote and ties require revision.

Decision record

Capture evidence reviewed, dissent or reservations, owners, and actions before the next gate.

AI rules of engagement

Use AI as a challenger, never as an unverified source

Log material prompts, summarize how the output affected the analysis, and identify the human-verified source used before any claim or number enters the committee record.

Permitted

Logic, debugging & challenge

Use AI to explain formulas, test reasoning, generate counterarguments, or surface missing risks.

Verify before use

Summaries, scenarios & estimates

Cross-check every factual claim and number against FactSet, FRED, SEC filings, or the team's workbook.

Not acceptable

Unverified decisions

Do not submit AI-generated allocations, risk metrics, citations, or client recommendations without human verification.
