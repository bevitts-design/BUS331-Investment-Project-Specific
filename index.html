Five-person committee

Five roles, one committee decision

Each member leads a workstream, but every member reviews, votes, signs the decision record, and answers questions across the full recommendation.

Committee seat

Client and Macro Strategist

Own client discovery, the 12-month market view, IPS objectives and constraints, and the handoff from client needs to investment criteria.

Committee seat

Fixed-Income Analyst

Own the income, capital-preservation, maturity, duration, credit-quality, liquidity, and tax questions that shape the fixed-income sleeve.

Committee seat

Equity Analyst

Own equity-fund and ETF due diligence and the limited use of individual equities, including exposure, style, sector, holdings, costs, liquidity, overlap, idiosyncratic risk, and position-size evidence.

Committee seat

Portfolio Manager

Own portfolio integration: resolve overlap and concentration, set final selection and weights, record explicit trade-offs, maintain decision-log quality, and move the integrated recommendation to a vote.

Committee seat

Risk and Derivatives Analyst

Own portfolio-level risk diagnosis, stress testing, residual-risk evidence, mandate-breach escalation, and the hedge or no-hedge conclusion; do not manufacture a derivative position when no targeted hedge is justified.

Committee operating protocol

Every phase ends with a decision

Analysis becomes committee work only when the team challenges it, votes, and records what happens next.

Pre-read

Circulate the required evidence before the meeting. Every member reviews the full package.

Recommendation

The workstream lead states a clear recommendation and cites the evidence that supports it.

Independent challenge

The Portfolio Manager coordinates the strongest counterargument; the analyst whose judgment is challenged must respond with evidence, and the Risk and Derivatives Analyst independently tests the residual risk.

Motion and vote

The phase lead states the motion. The Portfolio Manager records approve, revise, or reject; each member casts one vote, even if that member holds two roles, and any non-approval vote requires revision.

Decision record

Capture evidence reviewed, dissent or reservations, owners, and actions before the next gate.

AI rules of engagement

Use AI as a challenger, never as an unverified source

Log material prompts, summarize how the output affected the analysis, and identify the human-verified source used before any claim or number enters the committee record.

Permitted

Logic, debugging & challenge

Use AI to explain formulas, test reasoning, generate counterarguments, or surface missing risks.

Verify before use

Summaries, scenarios & estimates

Cross-check every factual claim and number against FactSet, FRED, SEC filings, or the team's workbook.

Not acceptable

Unverified decisions

Do not submit AI-generated allocations, risk metrics, citations, or client recommendations without human verification.
